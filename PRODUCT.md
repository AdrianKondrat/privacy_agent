# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, React and TypeScript. Deployment target is open.

## Users

Adults who have just had a reason to worry about their personal data: a breach email, a surge of scam calls, finding their address in a search, a fraud scare. Not security professionals.

## Product Purpose

Collect launch-list emails for a personal data leak fixer. The planned service finds where a person’s data got out along six paths (stolen, sold, shared, published, posted, indexed), fixes what can be fixed, and keeps a dated record of each fix. Strategy, PRD and plan are in `docs/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The launch pages collect an email address and, optionally, a broad region and the visitor’s main worry. The product is pre-launch. Demonstrations are fictional and no scan or removal is performed. Planned requests are sent only after the person approves them. Do not imply unattended submissions, whole-web coverage, guaranteed removals or being deleted from the internet.

## Brand Commitments

Koverr is the working name. Exposure reads in black and white; taking a protective step brings colour back.

## Evidence on Hand

`docs/` in this repository, and the earlier [AI privacy agent](https://app.notion.com/p/3f543965b45d80b78b5fef8e5d43568b) pages. No validated conversion, testimonials or customer outcomes yet.
