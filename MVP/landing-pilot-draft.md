## How the pilot works

1. **Check selected sources.** Start with 192.com and ukphonebook.com. The deeper check also covers Google Search. You enter your search terms on each site.
2. **Review a possible match.** Compare the listing with your own details. Mark a match as yours only if you can confirm it.
3. **Take action.** Use the official removal form. Record the request, reply, and any follow-up in Koverr.

## Pilot questions

**Where is the pilot available?**

The pilot is for people in the UK. You need a pilot invitation and an email code to sign in.

**Which sources does Koverr check?**

The free audit covers selected public sources. Each source controls the details it makes available. If a source blocks a check, you can record a coverage gap.

**Who sends the removal request?**

You submit the request on the organisation’s site. Koverr shows you the official steps and helps you record the outcome. The organisation decides how to respond.

**What happens when I find no matches?**

You can record that the sites you checked showed no matches. A listing can still appear elsewhere.
